FAQ

Quick answers.

Who can order?

Anyone with an assignment, project, presentation or creative requirement can enquire — school, college, university, business and other professional needs.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes. The page is positioned for Pan-India digital delivery. Physical delivery can be offered separately where available.

Can I request a specific style?

Yes. You can share references, colours, formats, examples or a Pinterest/Instagram style direction in your brief.

What are your assignment page rates?

Simple neat assignments start at ₹15/page, colourful neat assignments start at ₹20/page, and high-quality presentation pages are ₹50/page. Diagrams, flowcharts, graphs, tables and special requirements may cost extra depending on complexity. We confirm the final quote before production.

Can I get revisions?

Revision terms should be confirmed with the quote. Minor scope-aligned corrections can be included; major new requirements may change the quote.

How do I pay?

First, we confirm your scope and quote. Payment can then be collected through the payment method shared with you. Never share card details, UPI PINs or banking credentials with anyone.
